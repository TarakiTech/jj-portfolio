About

Engineering discipline, applied to AI automation, QA testing, and IT support.

Joel Jacob is an AI Automation Specialist, QA Automation Engineer, and IT support professional with over 16 years of engineering-grade discipline behind every workflow he builds, a foundation forged across semiconductor manufacturing in the Philippines and maritime electronics production in Singapore.

He designs and connects automated workflows using Zapier, Make, n8n, and HighLevel, and integrates AI platforms like ChatGPT, Claude, and Gemini into everyday business processes. The same structured problem-solving that once drove root cause analysis and CAPA now drives clean, reliable automation logic and dependable technical support.

On the quality side, sixteen years of test development sits behind his QA work, writing and executing test procedures for analog, digital, and embedded systems, owning Factory Acceptance Testing, and running defect triage through 8D and CAPA. That foundation now extends into Playwright-based UI test automation, where the same coverage thinking applies to software instead of hardware.

Joel also brings hands-on IT support proficiency, hardware and software troubleshooting, OS-level diagnostics and configuration, networking and Wireshark fundamentals, basic Python/C++ scripting, and smart-system integration across Google Home, Home Assistant, and Apple HomeKit, making him equally comfortable automating a workflow, debugging a failing test, or resolving an end-user ticket.
